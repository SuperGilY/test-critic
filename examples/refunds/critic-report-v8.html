Details
  • What is wrong in each test
  • T19: "Write down what happens (refund made or refused)". No pass or fail.
  • T08: two scenarios in one test. Quote: "Ask for a refund of -100". Zero is a separate step.
  • T16, T17: "Wait a few minutes, then look again". The spec says "The delay is not stated" (E4).
  • T20: the refund is already done on this card (E7: "refunds succeed immediately"), so a refusal may not come from R24a.
  • T24: relies on -1 being invalid. Quote: "ASSUMPTION: step 1 fails at validation, so the key is free."
  • T25: "If one was refused, send it again". A branch inside the test.
  • T07, T25: a pass proves nothing if the two requests were not at the same moment.
  • T12: "Can I run it now: Yes", but the spec marks the metadata call "[unverified syntax]".
  • T13, T14: "Yes", but V3 is marked "[same pattern as V1 and V2, page not opened]".
  • T21: step 3 uses the charge ID, but "Before you start" only says to write down the refund ID.
  • T13: marked Medium. Money would move on a payment that was never captured. It should be High.
  • What is wrong in the plan as a whole
  • P1 and its variants hold 27 of 29 tests. The card form is marked "[unverified]" in the spec.
  • 20 rules have no test and the plan does not say why.
  • Rule IDs match the spec. No ID was split or invented.
  • Tests that are missing
  • Refund on a cancelled PaymentIntent (R27c).
  • Refund again after a refund failed or was cancelled.
  • Refund amount with decimals or letters (R3c).
  • A key of 255 and 256 characters (R34).
  • A repeated or out-of-order event (R32).
  • Check for the reversal field on a quick refund (R28).
  • Vague rules in the spec
  • R24b: "Some card refunds can be cancelled for a short time". Which ones.
  • R29: "about 5-10 business days". Cannot be tested in test mode.
  • What the tester needs
  • Test-mode secret key (tester). Without it nothing runs.
  • Two terminal windows (tester). Without them T07 and T25 prove nothing.
  • Dashboard access in test mode (tester). T21 and T29 do not run without it.
  • A way to make a limited key, a pending balance, a yen charge (spec owner or tester). T26, T28, T27 stay blocked.
  • Risks someone must accept
  • 24 of 51 rules are not covered. Owner: QA lead. If nobody accepts this, they are never tested.
  • Many expected error texts are guesses marked ASSUMPTION. Owner: spec owner.
